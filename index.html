1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which BlueAppsBox ("we", "our", or "us") operates the mobile game Block Blast - Tomato Block Puzzle (Package Name: com.blueapps.tomatoblockpuzzle).

We believe in user transparency. This Privacy Policy informs players about our data practices, offline storage mechanics, and third-party advertising integration in strict compliance with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement and Google Play Developer Program Policies.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect (Zero PII Policy)

BlueAppsBox strictly enforces a zero Personally Identifiable Information (PII) policy. When you download, install, and play Block Blast - Tomato Block Puzzle, we do NOT ask for, collect, or store:

  • Personal Details: Your real name, home address, date of birth, or identification documents.
  • Contact Information: Phone numbers, email addresses (unless you voluntarily contact us via email for support), or address books.
  • Hardware Sensor Data: Camera, microphone, audio recording, or biometric data.
  • Location Data: Precise GPS coordinates or physical location history.
  • Financial Information: Credit/debit card details, banking credentials, or payment histories.

3. Local Storage Data (On Your Personal Device)

To provide a seamless block puzzle experience that functions entirely offline, the game uses standard local device storage (HTML5 LocalStorage / Android SharedPreferences).

  • High Score & Session Points: Stored locally to record your highest achievements.
  • Unlocked Levels (Levels 1 to 300): Stored locally so you can continue from where you left off.
  • Sound Settings: Saved locally so your audio preferences (sound on or muted) are remembered.

This information is never transmitted to, uploaded to, or shared with external servers by BlueAppsBox. It stays completely confined within your personal device sandbox.

4. Third-Party Advertisements & Google AdMob

To support free gameplay and provide voluntary revive features, Block Blast - Tomato Block Puzzle integrates Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob).

Google AdMob may process non-sensitive technical identifiers to deliver ads and prevent ad fraud, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID): A randomized, user-resettable identifier for advertising purposes.
  • Technical Device Telemetry: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and carrier network type.
  • Ad Interaction Metrics: Impressions, click events, and ad load error diagnostics.

Google's use of advertising data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or limit ad personalization at any time through your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads.

  • We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy requirements.
